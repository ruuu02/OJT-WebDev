Strawberry Yogurt Oats

Refreshing oat bowl with strawberries and tangy yogurt.

Nutrition Per Serving

Servings: 2 servings

Calories: 300 kcal / serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Quick Cook Oats
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup sliced strawberries
  • 1 tsp honey

Procedures

  1. Cook oats with milk until soft.
  2. Cool slightly and fold in yogurt.
  3. Top with strawberries.
  4. Drizzle honey and serve.
