Chilled Fruit Drink

A refreshing powdered drink for hot afternoons.

A refreshing powdered drink for hot afternoons.

Ingredients
4
Steps
4
Serves
1 pitcher
Level
Easy

Ingredients

  • Powdered drink mix
  • Cold water
  • Ice
  • Fruit slices

Instructions

  1. 1
    Pour mix into cold water.
  2. 2
    Stir until dissolved.
  3. 3
    Add ice.
  4. 4
    Garnish with fruit slices.
